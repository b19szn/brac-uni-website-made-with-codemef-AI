About Us

Where Purpose Meets Excellence

Founded in 2001 by BRAC, the world's largest NGO, we carry forward a legacy of empowering communities through education.

Academic Rigor

Curriculum designed with global standards, accredited by UGC Bangladesh and recognized internationally.

Research Excellence

Six dedicated research centers driving innovation in climate change, public health, data science, and social development.

Global Community

Partnerships with 100+ universities worldwide enabling student exchange, joint research, and dual degree programs.

Social Impact

Every graduate completes 100+ hours of community service, continuing BRAC's mission of poverty alleviation.

Our Founding Vision

BRAC University was established not merely as an academic institution, but as a catalyst for societal transformation. Sir Fazle Hasan Abed, founder of BRAC, envisioned a university that would produce graduates equipped with both professional competence and a deep sense of social responsibility.

Today, that vision manifests in our interdisciplinary approach, where engineering students collaborate with public health researchers, where business majors engage with development studies, and where every discipline is viewed through the lens of real-world impact.

Admissions

Your Journey Starts Here

Join a competitive, diverse community of scholars. We evaluate holistic potential, not just test scores.

01

Check Eligibility

Minimum GPA 2.5 in SSC & HSC (or equivalent). Specific requirements vary by program. International qualifications accepted (A-Levels, IB, SAT).

02

Online Application

Apply at admission.bracu.ac.bd. Submit academic transcripts, NID/Birth Certificate, passport photo, and application fee (BDT 1,500).

03

Admission Test

Written test (English, Math, Analytical) + Viva for selected programs. Test centers in Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, Rajshahi, Khulna.

04

Result & Enrollment

Results published within 2 weeks. Successful candidates complete enrollment, course registration, and orientation within the deadline.

Scholarships & Financial Aid

Merit Scholarship

Up to 100% tuition waiver for top 5% in admission test. Renewable with CGPA 3.75+.

Need-Based Aid

25-75% tuition support based on family income. 30% of students receive assistance.

BRAC Staff Quota

Reserved seats & tuition discounts for children of BRAC employees (10+ years service).

International Student Aid

Merit-based scholarships for SAARC & international students. Separate admission track.
